Use FreeCAD to model an L-shaped shelf bracket for 3D printing. Both legs are 80 mm long and 25 mm wide, 5 mm thick, with a 45-degree triangular gusset 5 mm thick in the middle of the inside corner, 40 mm along each leg. Each leg has two 4.5 mm countersunk screw holes, 20 mm and 60 mm from the outer corner, centred across the width. Put a 3 mm fillet on the inside corner. Print it flat on one leg.
