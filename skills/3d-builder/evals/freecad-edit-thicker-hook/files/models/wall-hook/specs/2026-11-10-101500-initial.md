## Request

> Make a printable wall hook for a heavy coat, screwed to the wall with two screws.

## Summary

A single-piece coat hook printed flat on its back plate, held by two 4 mm wood screws.

## Requirements

| Feature | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Back plate | 30 × 80 × 5 mm | decision |
| Hook arm length | 45 mm | decision |
| Hook thickness | 6 mm | assumption |
| Hook tip height | 15 mm | assumption |
| Screw holes | 2 × 4.5 mm, 15 mm from each end | decision |

## Decisions

1. **Should it hold a heavy coat?** → Yes
2. **Two screws?** → Yes, 4 mm wood screws

## Assumptions

- Hook thickness 6 mm: enough for a coat in PETG at 40% infill.
- Tip height 15 mm: keeps the coat loop from sliding off.
